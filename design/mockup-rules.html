Registry scores

criticality_score
1 to 10, 10 is most critical. Starting point for impact. notes: Registry scores
reliability_score
1 to 10, 10 is highly reliable. Runs opposite to likelihood; new starters routinely invert it. Starting point for likelihood. notes: Registry scores

Neither is sufficient alone; the finding text moves both. A reliable item with a crack in it is still cracked.

General

Likelihood

Concerns the item itself, given what the inspection found; how likely it is to stop performing its function in the near term. notes: The two questions

Moves it upnotes: What moves likelihood

  • A trend that is still moving
  • A repeat of a failure that has already occurred this period
  • An active, unmitigated mechanism; corrosion under wet insulation is progressing now
  • Detection by a method that only catches late-stage damage; a finding heard or smelled is further advanced than one found by UT
  • A confirmed functional failure that has already occurred sits at the top of the rangebrief: likelihood definition

Moves it downnotes: What moves likelihood

  • Design margin
  • A measurement inside its acceptance criterion
  • An item that is out of service and not required to be in service

Impact

Concerns what happens if it does fail; depends on what the equipment does and what sits behind it, not on the wording of the finding. notes: The two questions

Edited in v3

Scope. The consequence of the failure mode this finding points to, not the worst thing this equipment could ever do; a finding that touches only appearance, labels or records has housekeeping consequences even on a critical item.

inferredFrom the down list below; the first run scored an illegible PSV tag impact 8.

Moves it upnotes: What moves impact

  • Absence of redundancy
  • A Safety Critical Element, judged against the major accident it protects against, not repair cost
  • A failure that degrades a protection layer rather than causing direct loss
  • Delayed or hidden consequences; loss of corrosion inhibitor has no effect today and a substantial effect in eighteen months
  • Escalation potential

Moves it downnotes: What moves impact

  • Redundancy that is available and capable; if findings exist against both legs of a pair in the same period, the pair is not redundantnotes: What moves impactnotes: Two recurring errors
  • An item that can be bypassed without loss of function
  • Atmospheric, non-hydrocarbon, low-energy service
  • Consequences limited to housekeeping or appearance

Pitfallsnotes: Two recurring errors

  • Scoring by how severe the description soundsnotes: The two questions
  • Inverting reliability_scorenotes: Registry scores
  • Crediting redundancy without checking the partner; a partner with a finding this period is not a backup
  • Treating one head lost from a voted group as a defeat; it is a degradation, impact moderate, and time-limited because the next failure defeats it
  • Resolving uncertainty by picking a mid-range scorenotes: Output expectations

Output expectations

Summary
Covers what is wrong, on what, and why it matters. Readable without the inspection record and not a restatement of the finding text. Never omits a measurement, a repeat occurrence, or a standby already unavailable. notes: Output expectations
Recommended action
Names a specific activity; "investigate further" is not sufficient. brief: section 3
Uncertainty
State it explicitly; never resolve it with a mid-range score. notes: Output expectations

Loss of confidence, worst first

  1. Confidently incorrect scores on safety critical equipment
  2. A summary that omits a decision-changing detail
  3. The same finding scored differently on different days
  4. Uniform mid-range scores across all findings

Equipment types

One section per equipment type in the registry. The model reads only the section that matches the finding.

Centrifugal CompressorK-3401

  • Single train, no spare, no workaround; a trip puts the platform on recycle and cuts production within the hourregistry: K-3401
  • Vibration is trended daily; anything unusual gets a specialist the same dayregistry: K-3401
  • A bearing with a vibration trend that has not plateaued is high likelihoodnotes: The two questions

Reciprocating PumpP-3305

  • On corrosion inhibitor duty, losing the pump does not stop production, but inhibitor starvation starts corrosion in downstream carbon steel within weeksregistry: P-3305
  • The consequence lands six months later on someone else's desk; a delayed consequence still moves impact upregistry: P-3305notes: What moves impact

Pressure VesselV-1201, V-1305, V-2450

  • A hairline coating blister on a thick-walled vessel is low likelihood even on a critical vesselnotes: The two questions
  • On a pressure boundary vessel, any indication of wall loss, cracking or weeping goes to the integrity engineer regardless of sizeregistry: V-1201
  • A vessel only used during well testing, which can be isolated and bypassed indefinitely with no production impact, can usually wait for the next planned shutdownregistry: V-1305

Pressure Safety ValvePSV-1310, PSV-3420

  • Any evidence of passing or weeping is an impairment of a protection layer, not a maintenance nuisanceregistry: PSV-1310registry: PSV-3420
  • A regulatory clock runs on an impaired relief device from the moment it is knownregistry: PSV-1310

Gas DetectorFG-0455

  • Calibration drift from salt and humidity is common and mostly benign; a head that fails to respond to test gas is not benignregistry: FG-0455
  • In a voted group, one failed head is degradation with no margin leftnotes: Two recurring errors

Level TransmitterINST-2150, INST-2151

  • Duty and backup are a duplicated pair installed after a near miss; keeping both legs healthy is the whole pointregistry: INST-2150registry: INST-2151
  • Operators knowing when a transmitter lies is not a control measure that can be written downregistry: INST-2150

Firewater PumpFW-8101, FW-8102

  • Failure to start on demand is the scenario the whole fire protection philosophy is built to avoidregistry: FW-8101
  • Both pumps unavailable at once means production shutdownregistry: FW-8101
  • Battery and starting air problems recur on the older pump; it is historically the first to fail a test startregistry: FW-8102

Deluge SystemDEL-8300

  • Partial nozzle blockage reduces coverage over a specific area; the system looks healthy from the skid but is not delivering where it mattersregistry: DEL-8300

LifeboatLB-4102

  • Any defect that could prevent launch reduces the total evacuation capacity, which is compared against the POB count; that comparison is not negotiableregistry: LB-4102

Pedestal CraneCR-5001

  • Wire rope and hook inspections are governed by the lifting standard, a separate certification regimeregistry: CR-5001
  • Out of service means no supply boat offloads, a logistics problem within daysregistry: CR-5001

HVAC UnitHVAC-7201

  • The temporary refuge pressurisation unit keeps people mustering there from breathing hydrocarbons during a gas release; it is not building servicesregistry: HVAC-7201

StructureSC-1001

  • Coating breakdown reports are frequent and mostly cosmetic; fabric maintenance is chronically behindregistry: SC-1001
  • Section loss at a node or a cracked weld at a primary connection is a completely different severity and is never batched with the coating backlogregistry: SC-1001

PipingPL-1002

  • Live hydrocarbon line at full wellhead pressureregistry: PL-1002
  • Corrosion under insulation is the known threat and is invisible until the cladding comes offregistry: PL-1002

Urgency

Urgency is computed in code, never by the model. The notes give the shape; the numbers are choices. inferrednotes: Urgency

Bands
Today9 to 10
This week7 to 8
This month5 to 6
Next planned shutdown3 to 4
Backlog1 to 2
Weights
0.7 impact, 0.3 likelihood
Near-certain gate
Likelihood at least 9 lifts the base to at least impact
Inconsequential cap
Impact at most 3 caps urgency at 4
SCE floor
Impact at least 7 on an SCE floors urgency at 7

Overrides

Overrides lift the derived urgency to a floor; they never lower it. Floor 9, not 10, so two today items can still be ordered. notes: Urgency

  • protection_layer_impaired_no_deviation

    When a protection layer is impaired and no deviation is recorded

    Because the installation is out of compliance from the moment the impairment is known

    Effect urgency at least 9

  • evacuation_below_pob

    When evacuation capacity is reduced below the POB count

    Because same compliance logic as an impaired protection layer

    Effect urgency at least 9
